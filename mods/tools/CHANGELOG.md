## 21.1.0.0

First release for Minecraft 1.21.1, for NeoForge and Fabric. Releases for 1.21.1 are numbered 21.1.x.y, where 21.1 is the Minecraft version.

- Matches Assorted Tools 13.0.2 for Minecraft 26.3
- Includes Assorted Gear Sets, Assorted Hammers, Assorted Multitools, Assorted Machetes, Assorted Shears, Assorted Portable Workbench, Assorted Throwing Spears, Assorted Boomerangs, Assorted Buckets, Assorted Suits, Assorted Wands, Assorted Staffs, Assorted Ultimate Fist and Assorted Pokeball, each at 21.1.0.0
- Requires Assorted Lib 21.1.0.0

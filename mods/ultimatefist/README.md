# Assorted Ultimate Fist

Adds the ultimate fist. Its fragments are hidden in chests around the world, and with a nether star they make a fist that hits and digs harder than anything else.

Also included in [Assorted Tools](../tools). Worlds made with Assorted Tools 11.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).
